Not the real site. Prices are an invented "duck curve" (cheap at night and at midday, peaks in the morning and evening); the house, the sun and the planner are simulated. Assumptions: battery 30 kWh (10 % to 100 %), full charge or discharge rate 4.6 kW, exported energy is paid at 90 % of the spot price, the demo phase limit is 16 A (the site limit is 32 A). The mode words are those of docs/inverter-control-settings.md: 0x8000 Load First, 0xA000 Battery First, 0xC000 Grid First (only the first two were tested on the site).
